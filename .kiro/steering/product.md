# Product Overview

This is the website for **Dry Fabrications**, a software development company that
builds custom solutions for businesses. It is a single-page, informational site
rendered in an ASCII / terminal aesthetic. The tone is professional, not indie.

## Positioning
- Dry Fabrications is **primarily a software development company**, offering custom
  software solutions to businesses (internal tools, automation, full applications).
- It has also been **contracted to assist with game development**, and has begun
  development on its **own original game title**.
- Present the company professionally. Software services are the core; game work is
  an extension of that expertise, not the headline identity.

## Goals
- Inform visitors about the company, what it does, its project work, and contact details.
- Stay simple: a static site, no backend, no build step.
- Present a distinctive plain-text / terminal look.

## Site structure (single-view navigation)
- The site is a single HTML page with three content sections: **about, projects, contact**.
- Only one section is visible at a time. Clicking a nav link swaps the centered content.
- The page **defaults to the About section** on load.
- The URL hash reflects the active section (e.g. `#projects`) so sections are deep-linkable.
- The title "Dry Fabrications" sits in the top-left; content panels are centered.

## Important content facts
- Company name is written "Dry Fabrications" (normal capitalization).
- Founded in 2021.
- Contact is handled through a form in the Contact section (no email address is listed on the site).
- The Projects section features the studio's own game title, "SWAT: Guardian"
  (in development). Game-specific detail lives in the SWAT Guardian project, not here.

## Scope / preferences
- **No decorative ASCII art.** ASCII characters are used only for layout/formatting
  (box borders like `+---[ SECTION ]---+`, `|` edges, dividers, line breaks).
- Keep information progressive — not everything is shown at once.
